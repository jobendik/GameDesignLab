How to Play

Core Loop

Drag from the launcher to aim upward. Release to fire all balls. Bricks lose one health per hit. Destroy them before they cross the glowing danger line.

Collect +1 pickups to permanently add balls. The first returning ball sets the next launch point.

Special Nodes

Bomb nodes explode. Laser nodes cut rows or columns. Fire nodes empower shots. Chests give coins. Shielded nodes need their shell broken first.

Desktop: Space shoot, P/Esc pause, M mute, R restart.
