## Original concept and core mechanics
"Jump": tap to jump between scrolling platforms. A yellow square on green bars, no art, no sound, no progression.

## Baseline assessment
A bare auto-scroller placeholder; and the genre is already covered by Frostfoot (runner), Hover Hop (pad hopping) and
Pine Kick (wall kicks).

## Weaknesses identified
No identity, no feel, no difficulty curve, no hook — and too close to its siblings if merely polished.

## Creative opportunities
Keep the verb (jump over the obstacle) but change everything around it: nothing scrolls; the obstacle is a skipping rope
turned by two pals, so the game becomes rhythm, and the rope can *be* the soundtrack.

## Planned improvements
Rope as a schedule of passes (so tempo, doubles and a second rope are just different schedules); tap hop / hold big jump;
quality judged by foot height at the pass; groove generated by the passes; double-unders, flies, Double Dutch.

## Implemented improvements
- **Scene**: Pip (left) and Bun (right) turn a rope with wooden grips; Momo skips on a chalk-doodled playground mat;
  Olli and Nori watch and cheer once the streak hits 10. The rope is a 3D-ish loop: it rises behind Momo, drops in front
  (thicker, brighter, with a motion trail) and slaps the mat with a dust puff.
- **Controls**: tap / click / Space = hop (0.4 s airtime); hold = variable big jump (~0.65 s). 130 ms jump buffer on landing.
- **Judging**: at each pass Momo's feet must be off the mat; their height grades it Perfect / Good / Close (3/2/1) ×
  streak multiplier (×1–×4, every 10 skips).
- **Rhythm & audio**: every pass plays a kick + a bass note walking I–V–vi–IV; clean skips add a melody note that climbs
  with the streak (Perfect an octave above Good); hi-hats join on the rope's top from speed 3. The turners count in with
  "3, 2, 1, Skip!" ticks. No generative music: the rope is the band, so the beat is always exactly the gameplay beat.
- **Escalation**: speed tiers every 10 skips (1.1 s → 0.52 s per turn) with "Faster!" call + banner; from 14 skips Pip
  shouts "Double! Hold!" one turn ahead, the rope glows gold and whips round twice (0.36 s) — only a held jump clears both
  (+10×); flies hover above Momo's head (hold to snap one, +5×) at the risk of mistiming; every 40 skips Bun brings a
  second teal rope: Double Dutch, two ropes alternating for 14 passes (+20× on clearing). Skies go meadow → golden → candy.
- **Fail**: three hearts; a tangle shakes, Momo goes dizzy, Bun says "Oopsie!" and they count back in. +1 heart every 25
  streak. Card: "Tangled up!" with skips, best streak, double-unders.
- Attract mode: a bot skips behind the splash (same input functions).

## Test results
- Automated-gameplay: `tests/PhoneGameLab2/skip-jam.test.js` — count-in, four real touch taps timed to the rope clear four
  passes and score 1–3 each, Space hops, standing still tangles (heart, streak reset, count back in), a real held mouse
  jump after the "Double!" call clears two passes (double-under bonus), Double Dutch adds a second rope and faster skips,
  three tangles end the run with a best score, Enter restarts cleanly. Passed 3/3 consecutive runs (~30 s).
- Automated-technical: `node tools/smoke.js skip-jam` passes, 61 fps.
- Visual inspection: 390×844, 844×390, 1280×800 in normal play, the "Double! Hold!" call and Double Dutch. Fixed: portrait
  scene sat too low with an empty sky (raised the mat, bigger pals, added Olli/Nori as spectators).
- Subjective playtest: informed judgement from code and scripted play, not a human playtest.

## Remaining issues
- Judging is on the game clock and sounds fire on the frame of the pass: on devices with high audio latency the groove
  may feel a touch late; there is no latency calibration.
- In landscape the rope arc leaves the top third of the stage mostly sky.
- The hold-jump for flies is optional and easy to ignore.
