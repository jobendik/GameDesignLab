PUPPET PANIC

String-pulling charades. Act out the secret word with a floppy marionette before the crowd reaches for the tomatoes.

  • Drag the head, a hand or a foot. Let go and the string holds.
  • Tap a held string to drop it.
  • The crowd guesses what it really sees — three flops and the show is over.

Keys: 1–5 pick a string · arrows move it · X drop · Space stand · P pause
