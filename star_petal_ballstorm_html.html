How to Play

Drag from the unicorn launcher to aim upward. Release to fire all pearls. Each hit lowers a gem's number by 1. Collect +1 stars to add permanent balls for the run. Chests give coins, bombs sparkle-detonate nearby gems, and rainbow rays clear columns.

When all pearls return, a new row arrives. Do not let any gem touch the glowing moon line.
