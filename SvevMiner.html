Svev Miner

A finished arcade mining game built from the original prototype idea. Drift through a neon asteroid sector, mine unstable crystals, dodge hostile drones, upgrade your rig, and bank ore at the extraction beacon before the sector becomes too dangerous.

Mission Loop

Mine ore until your cargo is full, return to the central beacon to bank it, then use credits for upgrades. Each successful delivery advances the wave and spawns richer — but more dangerous — asteroids.

Mine
Hold mouse or Space near asteroids. Overheating reduces laser efficiency.
Deliver
Return full cargo to the beacon to score, repair, and unlock the next wave.
Upgrade
Improve laser, engines, shields, and cargo capacity at the beacon.
Survive
Avoid drones, mine storm crystals carefully, and boost away from danger.
