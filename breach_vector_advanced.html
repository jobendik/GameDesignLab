Breach Vector

Advanced Tactical Action Prototype

A top-down tactical action prototype with line-of-sight stealth, emergency lighting, interactive terminals, locked security doors, destructible cover, explosive barrels, pickups, enemy roles, suppression, flanking and extraction.

Tactical visibility
When hidden, the level is dark and enemies outside your sight are obscured. When detected, emergency lights come on and the full combat arena becomes readable.
Mission flow
Hack terminals, unlock doors, neutralize command targets, collect supplies and extract. The objective is no longer just “kill everything”.
Controls
WASD move · Mouse aim · LMB fire · RMB grenade · R reload · Space dash · F takedown · E interact · 1-4 weapons · Shift slow walk · Tab debug
Design note
This is still abstract prototype art, but the systems are asset-ready: replace circles/rectangles with sprites later without changing the game loop.
